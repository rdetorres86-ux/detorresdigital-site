Three stages, one clear report at the end.

  1. 01

    Research

    I learn your product, your users, and your competition before forming any opinion.

  2. 02

    Audit

    I diagnose what's actually broken across UI, personas, journeys, or onboarding, and rank it by severity.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    A fixed-scope roadmap you approve before anything gets built.

One clear report. No guessing what to fix first.

What you get

Deliverables scoped to what's actually broken.

No two audits produce the same fix. Some products need a cohesive design system, others need real user personas, a mapped journey, an information architecture fix, or a rebuilt onboarding flow. I diagnose which one (or combination) applies to yours, then put it in writing as a fixed scope before anything gets built. If the audit turns up more than what's agreed, that becomes its own proposal.

Findings report

Every issue documented with an annotated screenshot, severity rating, and the reasoning behind it.

Fix roadmap

Issues sequenced by impact and effort, so you know what to fix this week versus what can wait a quarter.

Figma redlines

Concrete design direction for your highest-priority fixes, ready to hand to a developer or an AI coding tool.

AI build instructions

When the fix is structural, a written brief your team's AI tools can build from directly: personas, use cases, user journeys, information architecture, or a design system with tokens, whichever ones your product is actually missing.

Readout call

A walkthrough of the findings with your team, live, with room for questions before you start building.
